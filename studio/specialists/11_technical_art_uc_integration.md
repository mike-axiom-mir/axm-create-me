## Hourly lane

Pick one high-leverage integration gap, repair the smallest shared contract/tooling necessary, and prove the exact path with retained evidence. Do not centralize domain knowledge into UC merely for convenience. Maintain `studio/status/11_technical_art_uc_integration.md`.
