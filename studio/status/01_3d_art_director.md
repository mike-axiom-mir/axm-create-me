1. premature **divergence** — every new department invents an unrelated visual language; or

## Bounded action

Created:

`studio/direction/VISUAL_BASELINE_001.md`

The packet establishes a first cross-repo comparison gate rather than a final house style. It asks early serious proofs to preserve source identity, compare at least two materially different realizations where appropriate, and expose silhouette, proportion, large/medium/small hierarchy, material separation, functional emphasis and family identity under comparable views.

It also asks for the first review board to span three different problem classes:

- hard-surface;
- organic;
- environment-scale.

Existing UC finishes and form/motion machinery are treated as ingredients only, not as automatic studio canon.

## Why this was the highest-leverage visible lane

There is not yet enough department output to perform a truthful comparative beauty/polish review. Writing detailed weapon/character/building taste rules now would be invented direction. A small comparison contract gives all upcoming specialist work a common visual evidence surface without freezing the result prematurely.
