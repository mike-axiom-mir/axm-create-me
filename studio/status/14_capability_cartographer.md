## Coordination rule refinement — dependency dimensions matter during source migration

The existing studio evidence-dependency ledger remains sufficient. No new registry/service/schema is justified.

Refine its interpretation with this rule:

1. Record the exact source identity and the representation dimension changed by a proposed migration.
2. Preserve unchanged source facts separately from changed representation facts.
3. A consumer whose substantive claim depends on the changed representation must rebind and rerun before claiming the successor.
4. A consumer whose domain semantics depend only on unchanged facts may keep its algorithm/contract unchanged, but must still refresh exact source provenance before claiming successor evidence.
5. A downstream receiver that has not yet imported the successor stays truthful for its historical chain and **HELD** for successor-chain claims.
6. Numeric or visual similarity is supporting evidence, not authority substitution.
7. The receiving specialist decides the minimum adequate rerun for its own acceptance contract; Cartography maps the dependency but does not steal domain acceptance.

Building makes this rule materially stronger than the earlier Object authority-only case because the topology representation genuinely changes while many source semantics remain stable.
