1. `producer_repo_ref` is part of the evidence meaning even when the produced numeric payload is equal to another producer's payload.

5. Do not blanket-invalidate downstream consumers that are still exactly pinned to the old consumer. Their old-chain evidence remains truthful.

7. The receiving specialist decides whether a changed exact dependency requires only a receipt/identity rebind or a full rerun of observational evidence. Cartography does not invent that acceptance rule centrally.
8. Historical evidence stays attached to the exact producer authority, representation binding and proof context it actually tested.

This extends the previous ledger learning without adding another identity dimension. The change is on the **edge from producer authority to consumer**, not in the numeric payload itself.
