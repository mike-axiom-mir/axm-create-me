### HOLD — delivered motion naturalness

**`HOLD_FINAL_COMPACT_EAST_MOTION_NATURALNESS__REVIEWABLE_DELIVERED_SEQUENCE_DISPLAY_TARGET_DEVICE_PENDING`**

The current retained artifact contains three deterministic-seek PNGs plus a numerical real-loop trace. It does **not** contain a rendered wall-clock sequence showing the frames actually delivered through `AnimationPlayer.play()` over the loop.

The proof-host process-frame cadence is slower than the source slot cadence and does not observe every exact source key. That is important delivery evidence, but it is **not itself an Art defect** and it is not permission to retime or reshape the source. A source key can be valid yet not be present at a sampled presentation callback; conversely, a missing observer sample does not prove display-scanout loss.

Art therefore refuses both unsupported conclusions:

- do **not** call the motion natural/smooth merely because all exact keys are valid;
- do **not** retime the source merely because the proof-host observer misses some 31.25 ms slots.
