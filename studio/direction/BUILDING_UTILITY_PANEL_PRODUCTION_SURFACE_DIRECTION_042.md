## Bounded next visual-direction improvement

Building Materials should author **one** production-intent utility-panel surface successor on the already-proven owner UV/image path.

Visual constraints for that successor:

1. preserve the ochre service-panel family identity and its subordinate role inside the pavilion hierarchy;
2. replace the obvious regular checker/calibration read with a manufactured-surface read that is coherent at both current cameras;
3. prefer broad/medium-scale material variation over a dense symbol-like grid; do not increase contrast, density or brightness merely to guarantee detectability;
4. do not invent seams, fasteners, damage, decals, wear or geometric facts that the current source does not own merely to add detail;
5. preserve the exact receiver, service-face placement, current cameras, lighting and unrelated world state for the next comparison;
6. keep the existing checker as the diagnostic transport control, not as a hidden discarded predecessor.

Art does **not** prescribe shader implementation, texture generation method, atlas strategy, final texel density, Runtime format, or Environment adoption.
