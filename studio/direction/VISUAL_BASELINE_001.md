# Visual Baseline 001 — prove the family before scaling it

Status: ACTIVE ART-DIRECTION PACKET / NOT A HOUSE STYLE / NOT PRODUCT CANON

## Why this packet exists

The eleven design department repositories are currently empty starting bodies. Universal Creation already has real 3D creation evidence, selectable surface finishes, animation/LOD evidence, and newer camera/look structural templates, but that evidence does **not** establish one universal AXM visual style for every department.

The first visual risk is therefore premature divergence: each department could become internally coherent while the constellation as a whole becomes visually unrelated. The opposite risk is premature convergence: forcing one attractive existing proof, such as Parcel Imp, to become the style for animals, architecture, maps, characters, weather and every future game.

This packet creates the smallest shared visual proof gate before either failure happens.

## Shared first-proof requirement

Before a design department claims a stable visual direction, its first serious 3D proof should retain one editable source and show at least **two materially different visual realizations** from that same source where the domain permits it.

The comparison must preserve exact source identity and should expose these art-direction dimensions rather than hiding them behind detail:

1. **Silhouette** — readable primary shape at thumbnail / play-distance scale.
2. **Proportion** — deliberate exaggeration or realism is visible in the large forms, not only surface detail.
3. **Large / medium / small hierarchy** — detail density has a readable order; micro-detail cannot carry the design alone.
4. **Material separation** — important material families remain distinguishable under representative lighting without depending only on color.
5. **Functional emphasis** — the eye can find the domain-defining parts quickly: muzzle/grip, armor protection/joints, face/hands, creature head/limbs, building entry/mass, unit mobility/weaponry, etc.
6. **Family identity** — variants remain recognizably related without becoming recolors of one model.

## Existing UC evidence we may reuse, but not overclaim

Current UC evidence gives us useful starting machinery:

- selectable `realistic`, `comic-salvage`, `painted-adventure`, and `graphic-toon` surface finishes;
- reusable form controls such as taper, squash, oversized functional parts, asymmetry and explicit large/medium/small hierarchy;
- character/robot expression and pose machinery;
- animation timing, secondary motion, LOD/readability and fresh-import evidence;
- the Parcel Imp combined showcase and later functional wheel/parcel-motion evidence;
- source-bound lighting/look and camera/shot template contracts.

These are **ingredients and proof surfaces**, not a finished studio-wide visual language. UC's own boundaries already separate structural success, still-image inspection, animation/runtime evidence and aesthetic quality.

## First comparison board

The Art Director wants the first usable comparison board to contain at least three materially different domains once the relevant specialist lanes can produce them:

- one hard-surface subject (`weapon`, `armor`, `unit`, `building` or `object`);
- one organic subject (`character`, `animal` or `nature`);
- one environment-scale subject (`map`, `building`, `nature` or `weather` composition).

For each subject retain:

- editable source identity;
- neutral/reference realization where meaningful;
- one more stylized realization;
- same-view or source-bound camera comparison;
- at least one thumbnail/play-distance view;
- one closer craft view;
- explicit evidence label for what was actually inspected.

Do not use different cameras, hidden geometry edits or unrelated lighting to manufacture an apparent style win.

## Temporary art-direction bar

Until we have the first three-domain board, the studio should optimize for:

- strong readable silhouettes before dense ornament;
- intentional proportion before polygon count;
- clear functional storytelling before generic realism;
- material and value grouping that survives distance;
- authored asymmetry where it adds identity rather than noise;
- editable rich source retained underneath cheaper/stylized realizations;
- no assumption that `comic-salvage`, realism, toon, or any current proof is the final universal answer.

This is a **comparison bar**, not a taste mandate. A department may deliberately diverge when evidence shows its domain needs a different treatment.

## Rejection conditions

A first proof is not sufficient for visual-direction promotion when:

- the only visible change is color, texture density or polygon count;
- silhouette/proportion remain generic and the design relies on surface noise;
- the close render looks strong but the play-distance read collapses;
- a stylized realization silently changes source identity or function;
- different camera/lighting conditions make comparison meaningless;
- one successful asset is used to declare a department or style mastered;
- technical validity is presented as aesthetic acceptance.

## Handoffs

### Geometry & Topology
Preserve silhouette, proportion and large/medium/small hierarchy while establishing clean editable forms. Return evidence when technical cleanup materially changes the visible read.

### Hard-Surface / Organic Form / Environment
Produce the first domain-spanning comparison candidates rather than large catalogs. One strong, inspectable subject per domain is more useful than many unreviewed outputs.

### Materials / LookDev
Use surface treatment to support material separation and form readability. Do not hide weak forms under wear/noise. Preserve same-source comparisons.

### Technical Art / UC Integration
Expose a repeatable way to request or retain same-source multi-realization comparisons without centralizing domain identity into UC.

### Visual Observer / QA
Fail the comparison if views are not actually comparable, if images are missing, or if claims exceed direct observation.

### Capability Cartographer
Track which visual rules transfer across domains and which repeatedly fail. Only repeated evidence should graduate a temporary art-direction rule into shared studio capability.

## Promotion gate

This packet may be revised after the first real three-domain comparison board. Nothing here promotes an asset, style, profession or department to mastered/WORKING/CANON by itself.
