## Art Direction interpretation

The candidate is a clear visual improvement in this exact current-world proof because it restores a basic material-role hierarchy that the proof-only green response suppresses:

1. trunks and branches now read as one woody structural family instead of as the same bright green visual material as foliage;
2. foliage remains the greener accent layer rather than swallowing the full tree silhouette;
3. the Nature layer becomes less synthetic/debug-like and sits more naturally beside the accepted dark Building shell, neutral route and restrained Weather ribbons;
4. the change remains bounded to Nature material role and does not require a simultaneous form, placement, wind, lighting or Weather retune.

The candidate does **not** solve Nature form/detail quality. Foliage is still sparse and stylized, species identity remains weak, and no claim is made that these two scalar colors/roughness values are final production lookdev. Those are separate later questions.
