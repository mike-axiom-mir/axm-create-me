## Art hold — wall-clock motion exposes excessive bead regularity

Direct inspection of all `44` retained wall-clock frames exposes a presentation defect that fixed-sample seed review could not judge reliably.

Across the strongest active plateau, approximately `0.36–0.61 s`, both cameras repeatedly show many warm square billboards as a long countable near-linear chain. The repeated equal-ish billboard read plus coherent spacing makes the cue read closer to **rigid dotted beads / a segmented exhaust-like trail** than to a brief irregular release accent.

This is not a single-camera accident:

- continuity three-quarter shows the countable trail clearly through the middle active frames;
- left-oblique seam reproduces the same regular-chain read from the opposite side.

Art also ran a bounded diagnostic on the exact `0.40 s` static candidate-control delta masks. Using pixels above `1 LSB` and 8-connected components:

- continuity produces `16` visible effect components; their component-centroid cloud has about **99.7420%** of its variance on one principal axis;
- left-oblique produces `12` visible effect components; about **99.6156%** of centroid variance lies on one principal axis.

The component counts are below the `18` active motes because some raster regions overlap. This diagnostic is supporting evidence for the observed linear staging; it is **not** a universal VFX-quality metric or an acceptance threshold.
